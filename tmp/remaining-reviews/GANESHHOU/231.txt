NOTE - 36 Financial Risk Management:-
The Company’s activities expose it to market risk, liquidity risk and credit risk. In order to minimize any adverse effects
on the financial performance of the company, derivative financial instruments, such as foreign exchange forward
contracts and foreign currency option contracts are entered to hedge certain foreign currency risk exposures and
interest rate swaps to hedge variable interest rate exposures. Derivatives are used exclusively for hedging purposes
and not as trading or speculative instruments.
