2.14. Earning per share


      Basic earnings per share are calculated by dividing the net profit or loss for the period attributable to
      cquity shareholders (after deducting attributable taxes) by the weighted average number of equity shares
      outstanding during the period.
      For the purpose of calculating diluted earnings per share, the net profit or loss for the period attributable
       to equity shareholders and the weighted average number of shares outstanding during the period are
      adjusted for the effects of all dilutive potential equity shares.


2.15. Cash Flow statement


     Cash flows are reported using the indirect method, whereby profit before tax is adjusted for the effects of
       transactions of non-cash nature and any deferrals or accruals of past or future cash receipts or payments.
     The cash flows from operating, investing and financing activities of the Company are segregated based
      on the available information.
