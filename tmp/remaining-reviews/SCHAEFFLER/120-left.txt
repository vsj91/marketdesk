Nature and purpose of reserves:
a)   Capital reserve represents capital surplus. The reserve is not for any specific purpose but the utilisation will be in accordance with
     provision of Companies Act 2013.
b)   Securities premium is used to record premium received on issue of equity shares and it will be utilised in accordance with provisions of
     the Companies Act, 2013.
c)   General reserve is created out of profits earned by the Company by way of transfer from surplus in the Statement of Profit and Loss. The
    Company can use this reserve for payment of dividends and/or issue of fully paid-up shares.
d)   Retained earnings are the profits that the Company has earned till date, less any transfers to General reserve and payment of dividend. It
      is utilised in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013.
