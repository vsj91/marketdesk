Credit Risk
The Group’s customer proﬁle include Government Companies and Industries. Accordingly, the Group’s customer credit risk is
moderate. The Group has a detailed review mechanism of overdue customer receivables at various levels within organization
to ensure proper attention and focus for realization.
An impairment analysis is performed at each reporting date on an individual basis for major customers. In addition, a large
number of minor receivables are grouped into homogenous groups and assessed for impairment collectively. The Company
applies Simpliﬁed approach for providing the expected credit losses on Trade Receivables as per the accounting policy of the
company.
