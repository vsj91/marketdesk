#3. Staff welfare expenses

    Medical insurance plan - Cyient Inc:

    Cyient Inc., and Cyient Semiconductors Inc., offers a medical insurance plan to its associates on self-insured basis which
    consists of fixed costs of administration charges and stop loss insurance that are charged on a per associate basis and
    monthly claims being settled from consolidated fund maintained by third party insurance fund. At the end of every financial
     year, the insurance agency provides an estimate of “Claims Not Yet Received” computed on actuarial valuation based on
    number of associates and claims received over the last 12 months. Total expense recognised during the year aggregated
    to ₹ 678 ( March 31, 2025: ₹ 643).
