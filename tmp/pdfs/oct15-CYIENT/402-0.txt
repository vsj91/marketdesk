3.   In earlier years, a civil class action antitrust lawsuit was filed in a U.S. District Court (consolidating multiple civil lawsuits),
    against one of the Parent’s U.S. subsidiaries, Cyient Inc. amongst various other companies. It was alleged that the defendants
    agreed to restrict the employment of individuals. During the year ended March 31, 2024, Cyient Inc., a wholly owned
    subsidiary of the Company, had entered into an agreement to settle a civil class action antitrust litigation in the United
    States for an aggregate consideration of ₹ 676 ($ 8.1Mn), which was disclosed as an exceptional item in the consolidated
     financial statements for that year. During the year end March 31, 2025, the Court has granted preliminary approval of the
    agreement in January 2025. Accordingly, Cyient Inc. has paid $ 0.9 Mn net of an insurance claim of $ 3 Mn. A motion for final
    approval of the settlement has been filed on April 7, 2025 and balance amount is expected to be paid upon the final hearing.
    During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received an insurance claim of ₹ 207 towards the settlement of a civil
    class action antitrust litigation, which was concluded pursuant to Court approval. The proceeds have been classified under
    exceptional items.
