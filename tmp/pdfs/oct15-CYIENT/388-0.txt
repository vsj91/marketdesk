iii) Long Service Leave – Australia:

The regulations of long service leave are applicable to the associates of the Company employed at its Australia Branch and
subsidiaries. The accrual of long service leave is in addition to the compensated absences to which the associates are entitled to.
These long service leaves are dependent on the tenure of the employee with the same employer and are regulated by respective
state laws.
