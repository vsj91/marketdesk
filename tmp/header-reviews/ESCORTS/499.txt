     a.   Valuation process and technique used to determine fair value

                 (i)   The fair value of quoted equity shares is based on the current bid price of respective investment as at the balance
             sheet date.

                  (ii)  The fair value of investments in mutual fund units is based on the net asset value (NAV) as stated by the issuers
               of these mutual fund units in the published statements as at the Balance Sheet date. NAV represents the price
               at which the issuer will issue further units of mutual fund and the price at which issuers will redeem such units
             from the investors.

                  (iii)  The derivative financial instruments are valued using forward exchange rates as at the balance sheet date.
