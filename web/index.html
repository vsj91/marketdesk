How paper trading works

Experimental long-only paper strategies check every 15 seconds. New buys use the selected strategy’s entry window; exits run whenever the NSE normal session is open and prices are fresh. Settings survive restarts and manage all holdings in that portfolio. Keep this Mac awake and the server running. Research, refreshes and new buys cover the Nifty 500; index membership and the Trader index-trend filter are separate checks.

Fill model: fresh bid/ask depth (≤ 5 seconds), at most 10% of displayed shares per level, and a 0.5% price limit from the best quote. Unfilled shares are cancelled immediately. Itemized delivery-style cost estimates apply to both portfolios; same-day tax netting and settlement are not modeled. No depth means no fill. Corporate actions still need reconciliation.
