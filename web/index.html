How paper trading works

Live baskets. Each basket checks the market every 15 seconds while the NSE session is open. Buys and sells need a live price no older than 30 seconds and real bid/ask depth; nothing is sent to a broker. New buys cover the Nifty 500. Keep this Mac awake and the app running.

Fill model: fresh bid/ask depth (≤ 5 seconds), at most 10% of displayed shares per level, and a 0.5% price limit from the best quote. Unfilled shares are cancelled immediately. Delivery-style cost estimates apply to every basket; same-day tax netting and settlement are not modeled. No depth means no fill. Corporate actions still need reconciliation.

Archived replay information · Draft rules and tests

Multi-factor, Risk parity, Stat-arb and Regime switching. These four build their plan after the close from completed daily candles: which stocks to hold and how much. During the next market session the plan is carried out live, through the same checks as every other basket: a fresh price, real bid/ask depth, the fee limit and the drawdown breaker. Holdings are only resized when they drift more than 2 percentage points from target. Nothing is sent to a broker.

Known data limits: fundamentals are the latest annual report, used from 60 days after its period end; market cap is AMFI's six-month average scaled by price; dividend yield, sector indices and VIX are not available, so value uses earnings yield and book/price, stat-arb uses price Z-scores, and regimes use Nifty 500 realized volatility.
