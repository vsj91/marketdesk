    What changed from the daily test

    • All stocks that could qualify from prior daily inputs are considered. Daily indicators use only completed earlier sessions. No selection based on the old replay's winners or losers.
    • A completed minute generates a signal. A fill is attempted at the next consecutive minute's open with 10 bps adverse slippage, entry revalidation and estimated delivery charges. No same-bar close fills or guaranteed stop prices.
    • Primary scenario observes minute closes. The sensitivity scenario reacts after a completed minute touches a stop or target. If both are touched, the order is explicitly unknown; the fill still uses the next open. These are alternative assumptions, not best/worst bounds.
    • Fill size is capped at 10% of the preceding completed minute's volume and 1% of prior 20-session average daily volume per stock/day. These are liquidity assumptions, not order-book evidence. Unfilled quantities are cancelled; held remainders are checked again.
    • Entries execute 09:20–14:59 IST. Exits operate through the normal session, with overnight holdings and gap exposure. A final-minute signal waits for a fresh observation next session. Stale held prices block buys; the 2% daily buy halt persists for the session.
    • Current-minute volume is used only to identify whether any trade occurred, never to increase fill capacity. Missing or zero-trade minutes cannot produce synthetic fills. Provider timestamps denote candle start; close/high/low become known one minute later.
    • Current universe, possible later historical revisions, corporate actions and unmodeled taxes/settlement remain limitations. This is an execution-model test, not proof of a profitable strategy.
